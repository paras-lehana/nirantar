↺The loop learns and repeats: feedback (useful / not useful) and the alert policy change the next ranking; the next machine that drifts starts again at step 1.

The AI cannot approve

No path lets the AI approve, release, schedule or close a work order (policy POL-G8).

No crew for a broken sensor

A frozen or disagreeing sensor gets an inspection task, never a repair crew (SOP-50).

Safety before speed

Lock-out first; steps unlock only with the part on site; release only below 2.3 mm/s (ISO 10816 zone A).

Everything is logged

Every AI action and human decision goes to the audit log with who and when (ACTION_LOG in Snowflake).
