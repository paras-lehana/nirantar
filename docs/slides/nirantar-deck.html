Brief ② · Root cause in plain language

Ask why, and get evidence with its sources

Why does VMC-204 keep failing?

Bearing wear. Four related sensors moved together, so it is not a sensor fault. The two earlier breakdowns were tool-insert wear, a different fault. Repair per SOP-17; the part comes from Chennai.

Guardrails as features

Nothing is released without a person

POL-G8 · approval gate

The AI drafts; only a named person, through APPROVE_WORK_ORDER, releases work.

SOP-50 · sensor-fault veto

A frozen or disagreeing sensor gets an inspection, never a repair crew.

Refused and logged

"Release it without approval" is refused, and the refusal is logged.

Least privilege, capped spend

A role that owns one database, a resource monitor and CoCo credit caps.

Copilot: 15 of 15 golden questions, 820 phrasings

Every agent action and every refusal is written to APP.ACTION_LOG and shown on 8 · Trust Audit
