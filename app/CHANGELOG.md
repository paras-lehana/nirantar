## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-06
- Six new tools, grouped in the rail (Plan and act · Analyse · Run the demo · About):
  - **Maintenance calendar**: 7-day plan by line (agenda on phones), proposed vs booked windows, predicted failures, order
    due dates, preventive jobs due/overdue from the maintenance history, and *opportunistic maintenance* (add overdue jobs
    to a stop that is already planned, with a name).
  - **Technician view**: the phone job card. Lock-out first, steps locked until the part arrives, step ticks shared with
    Work Orders, release reading (≤ 2.3 mm/s), notes, local photo, English / हिन्दी.
  - **Reliability & root cause**: failure Pareto, MTBF / MTTR by line, bad actors, repeat failures, fishbone + 5 whys
    (data-confirmed vs hypothesis; "confirm at teardown" is logged).
  - **Compare machines**: a machine against its identical siblings (overlay chart, side-by-side table), fleet deviation
    heatmap, plant benchmark.
  - **Alarm rules**: ISA-18.2 what-if for alarm limits (alarms per day vs early-warning lead), proposals logged for
    management-of-change review (never applied to the model), notification routing preview.
  - **Business case**: illustrative payback calculator prefilled from the sample plant; works without data.
- Inbox bell: what the AI and the system did since you last looked.
- Works offline and installs as an app (manifest + service worker generated by `tools/build_sw.py`).
- Copilot: answers overdue preventive jobs (with bundling suggestions), sibling comparison and payback questions.
- Store: `pmPlan`, `schedulePM`, `toggleCheck`, `recordReading`, `addNote`; preventive jobs stay off Work Orders (step 5
  is for AI-drafted repairs) and show on the calendar; Shift brief lists them.
- Home: four role paths (plant head, planner, technician, engineer or judge). Help: 9 new glossary terms. How it is built:
  says which views are demo-only.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-06
- First public release: a browser-only twin of the Nirantar command center built in Snowflake, on synthetic data.
- Seeded world generator (3 plants, 48 machines, sensors with limits, ERP orders and spares, CMMS history, 56 days of
  failure history, 30 days of OEE) with 5 scenarios, Load sample data and Clear data.
- Model: health, failure confidence, time to failure with range, ISO 10816 zones, vibration spectrum with bearing
  defect frequencies, money at stake, repair windows, what-if odds read from the time-to-failure range, back-test.
- 8-step guided tour (Plant Map, Machine Detail, Alert Triage, What If, Work Orders, Ask Copilot, OEE, Trust Audit) plus
  Spares, Energy, Shift brief, Presenter, Data, How it is built and Help; welcome dialog, command palette, live clock.
- Human-in-the-loop approvals with an audit trail; copilot rule-based engine with guardrail (15/15 golden questions).
- Tests: `node app/tests/store.test.mjs`, `node app/tests/golden.test.mjs`; route sweep `python3 tools/appsweep.py`.
